<div class="profile-lead" markdown="1">
I am a **PhD student at [Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen](https://www.hitsz.edu.cn/)**, conducting research jointly with **[Peng Cheng Laboratory](https://www.pcl.ac.cn/)**. My work connects digital twins, immersive interaction, visual perception, and intelligent decision support for real-world systems.
</div>

I am advised by **[Prof. Hezhenyu](https://faculty.hitsz.edu.cn/hezhenyu)** from the School of Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, and **[Prof. Guiweihua](https://pcl.ac.cn/html/918/)** from Peng Cheng Laboratory. Previously, I worked as a researcher at **[The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/)** and the **[Shenzhen Institutes of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics for Society](https://airs.cuhk.edu.cn/)**, focusing on generative AI and extended reality. I also serve as a scientific advisor for AI companies working on metaverse, 3D vision, AI avatars, and immersive technologies.

My master's degree is from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, under the supervision of Prof. Lijinhui and Prof. Hezhenyu. My research has resulted in more than 10 papers and patents, and I regularly review for venues including NeurIPS, ACM MM, CVPR, ICCV, BMVC, ICML, and ACL.
