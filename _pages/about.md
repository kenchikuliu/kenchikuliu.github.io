<div class="profile-summary" markdown="1">
I am a **PhD student at [Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen](https://www.hitsz.edu.cn)**, conducting research jointly with **[Peng Cheng Laboratory](https://www.pcl.ac.cn/)**. My work focuses on **digital twins, XR systems, visual SLAM, 3D reconstruction, large language models, and human-AI interaction**.
</div>

I am advised by **[Prof. Zhenyu He](https://faculty.hitsz.edu.cn/hezhenyu)** from the School of Computer Science at Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, and **[Prof. Weihua Gui](https://pcl.ac.cn/html/918/)** from Peng Cheng Laboratory. Before my PhD, I worked as a researcher at **[The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://www.cuhk.edu.cn/)** and the **[Shenzhen Institutes of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics for Society](https://airs.cuhk.edu.cn)**, where I worked on generative AI and extended reality.

I am interested in building immersive, intelligent systems that connect perception, reasoning, and interaction in physical environments. Recent work spans multi-view stereo, robust visual SLAM, mixed-reality industrial collaboration, operational twins, and LLM-assisted teleoperation.
